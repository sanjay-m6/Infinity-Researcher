# Phase 14 Structured Paper Understanding Engine

## 1. Overview

The Paper Understanding Engine enables researchers to dissect complex manuscripts into structured dimensions on demand. Rather than generic open-ended summaries, the engine extracts domain-tailored research artifacts.

---

## 2. Structured Understanding Dimensions

### 2.1 Structured Summary
- **Core Problem**: Exact mathematical or conceptual challenge addressed.
- **Primary Objectives**: Clear list of targeted research aims.
- **Key Contributions**: Specific novel algorithmic or theoretical advances.
- **Proposed Method**: High-level architecture and pipeline summary.
- **Empirical Findings**: Benchmark performance gains with reported metrics.
- **Dual Limitations**: Clear segregation of author-admitted vs inferred limitations.
- **Future Directions**: Extracted roadmap for subsequent inquiry.

### 2.2 Methodology Deep-Dive
- **Research Design**: Study paradigm (e.g., empirical benchmarking, ablation, user study).
- **Theoretical Framework**: Governing formalisms and mathematical derivations.
- **Datasets & Materials**: Exact corpora, splits, and preprocessing procedures.
- **Baseline Comparisons**: Competing models and control baselines.
- **Experimental Protocol**: Hyperparameters, hardware clusters, and optimization steps.
- **Evaluation Metrics**: Formal loss functions and reporting metrics (e.g., BLEU, F1, Perplexity).
- **Reproducibility Assessment**: Evaluation of code/data availability and clarity.

### 2.3 Results & Empirical Metrics
- **Performance Benchmarks Table**:
  - Metric name (e.g., `Throughput TFLOPs`, `Accuracy`)
  - Reported value
  - Baseline value
  - Condition / Dataset split
- **Statistical Significance**: Inspection of reported p-values, confidence intervals, or standard deviations.
- **Ablation Insights**: Key empirical takeaways from layer or module removal.
- **Unexpected Outcomes**: Behaviors departing from initial hypotheses.

### 2.4 Limitations & Threats to Validity
- **Author-Acknowledged Limitations**: Boundaries directly conceded by authors in the text.
- **Inferred Methodological Vulnerabilities**: Independent peer-review scrutiny identifying potential design flaws.
- **Scope Boundaries**: Unaddressed real-world distributions or unproven domains.
- **Computational Constraints**: Memory footprints, latency bottlenecks, or GPU requirements.
- **Threats to Validity**: Construct, internal, and external validity threats.

### 2.5 Technical Terminology Explainer
- **Search & Query**: Interactive term lookup or click-through on common terms.
- **Paper Definition**: How authors define the term within their specific system.
- **Context in Paper**: Structural role of the concept in the proposed solution.
- **Simplified Explanation**: Plain-English intuitive breakdown for researchers new to the domain.
- **Related Concepts**: Clickable pills to explore adjacent terminology.
